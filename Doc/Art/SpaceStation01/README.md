## Scope and remaining validation

These are generated reconstruction references, not renders from a shared verified 3D mesh. A clean identity reference was generated first and supplied to every view. Front/back camera angles were corrected after the first pass. Final images still have some visible upper surfaces and are not strict calibrated engineering elevations; small seams, proportions, light placement, and occlusion may differ. No Tripo upload or 3D generation has been performed. Inspect reconstructed deck flatness, entry orientation, side-pod correspondence, and unwanted floating/disconnected parts before Unity use.

The source gameplay view does not establish the full stern or underside. Their completion, explicit port/starboard pod arrangement, and outboard utility assembly are this round's proposed interpretation, not previously approved hidden geometry.

Workflow references:
- [Tripo multi-view guidance](https://www.tripo3d.ai/help/features/how-to-use-multi-view-input-effectively): consistent design, lighting, background and scale.
- [Tripo legacy multi-view API](https://docs.tripo3d.ai/model-generation/multiview-to-model-v3-0-v3-1.html): positional order.
