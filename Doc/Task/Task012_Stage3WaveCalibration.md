# Task012 - Stage3 Wave Calibration

Status: Planned

Depends on: Accepted Task011 Stage2 Wave Calibration

Blocks: Task013, Task016, and Task017

## 1. Goal

Calibrate Stage3 around one developed Magic Core, persistent route-adjacent
contact, and useful coverage across more than one route zone.

## 2. Fixed Design Intent

- Archer, Cannon, and Magic pools;
- seven-Draft candidate budget;
- one L2 Magic Core with one Basic and one Behaviour Upgrade;
- three L1 Supports including Archer and Cannon;
- Anti-pattern concentrates effective coverage in one local area and leaves the
  remaining route under-covered.

## 3. Stage Composition And Fixed Build Fixtures

- Derive exactly seven Drafts from four Tower placements, one Core Level Up, and
  two Core Upgrade applications.
- Author the exact cumulative Archer/Cannon/Magic Tower and Upgrade pools and
  prove continuous Level 2 reachability for every family permitted to reach it.
- Select the repeated Support family, exact Magic Reference package, coherent
  alternative, single-zone Anti-pattern, and legal multi-zone placements.
- Author one seven-step naturally eligible Fixed Draft sequence for every
  required Build, preserving the Tower-only Initial step and the complete normal
  Draft, Pending, placement, Level Up, Upgrade, and consumption workflow.

Fixed sequences do not provide natural offer-frequency or player-preference
evidence; those questions remain Task017 work.

## 4. Stage Calibration And Monster Authoring

Author six positive Player Progress Requirements. Their sum owns the final-Draft
resolution node; the total Monster budget must preserve meaningful combat after
that node.

Reuse fixed-speed Profiles accepted by Task010-Task011 whenever they can express
Stage3's opening, persistent-contact, coverage, and late-pressure needs. Add a
new Profile only for an unmet Stage role. A new Profile is accepted here with an
explicit HP and `MoveSpeed = 0.25`; standard Waves retain
`SpawnInterval = 2.5s` and the `0.625` gap. Upstream Profiles are immutable unless
their introducing Stage and affected dependents are reopened.

Record the complete `MonsterWaveConfig` table and jointly calibrate Progress,
Profile order and Count, any new HP, `WaveDelay`, Player Health, placement, and
the exact legal Reference/alternative packages. Do not reduce Monster pressure
merely because Magic distributes damage rather than converting the same kill
count as another family.

## 5. Acceptance

- Magic's accepted contact identity produces measurable Core contribution;
- multiple route zones require useful Support placement;
- Reference and a coherent alternative clear within the reviewed margin;
- single-zone concentration fails from an explainable coverage gap;
- no Wave relies on speed differentiation before Task016;
- all seven Drafts occur at reviewed pressure nodes with a meaningful completed-
  Build window;
- exact Stage pools, Level reachability, legal Build fixtures, and Fixed
  sequences are accepted inside this Task;
- every Profile is identified as reused or first accepted here and the accepted
  Wave table matches the authored `MonsterWaveConfig`;
- Recorder separates delivered damage from kill conversion.

Fixed Draft sequences own all Build-comparison runs. Natural offer probability
and player-choice interpretation are deferred to Task017.
