# Task013 - Stage4 Wave Calibration

Status: Ready; Task012 Stage3 Wave Calibration accepted on 2026-08-28

Depends on: Completed Task012 Stage3 Wave Calibration

Blocks: Task014, Task016, and Task017

## 1. Goal

Calibrate Stage4 so a developed Drone Core and broader spatial coverage answer
the expanded route more reliably than only local fixed firepower.

## 2. Fixed Design Intent

- all four TowerFamilies available;
- eight-Draft candidate budget;
- one L2 Drone Core with one Basic and one Behaviour Upgrade;
- four L1 Supports representing Archer, Cannon, and Magic plus one reviewed
  repeated family;
- Anti-pattern relies on local fixed coverage that cannot answer the complete
  route.

## 3. Stage Composition And Fixed Build Fixtures

- Derive exactly eight Drafts from five Tower placements, one Core Level Up, and
  two Core Upgrade applications.
- Author the exact cumulative four-family Tower and non-Elemental Upgrade pools
  and prove continuous Level 2 reachability for every family permitted to reach
  it.
- Select the repeated Support family, exact Drone Reference package, coherent
  alternative, local-firepower Anti-pattern, and legal expanded-route placement
  assumptions.
- Author one eight-step naturally eligible Fixed Draft sequence for every
  required Build, preserving the Tower-only Initial step and normal Draft,
  Pending, placement, Level Up, Upgrade, and consumption behavior.

Fixed Build evidence is not natural probability or player-preference evidence;
Task017 owns that later calibration.

## 4. Stage Calibration And Monster Authoring

Author seven positive Player Progress Requirements. Their sum identifies the
final-Draft resolution node; the total Monster budget must retain meaningful
pressure after it.

Start with fixed-speed Profiles accepted by Task010-Task012. Reuse them where
they express Stage4's opening, expanded-route, and late-pressure roles. Add a new
Profile only for an unmet need, with explicit HP, `MoveSpeed = 0.25`, and standard
`SpawnInterval = 2.5s`. Do not mutate an upstream Profile without reopening its
introducing Stage and all affected dependents.

Record the full `MonsterWaveConfig` table. Calibrate Progress, Profile order and
Count, any new HP, `WaveDelay`, Player Health, exact legal packages, and placement
together. Drone pursuit value must be measured through active time, damage, and
route coverage rather than inferred from visual motion.

## 5. Acceptance

- opening allows the larger footprint plan to form;
- mid/late pressure spans route zones that make pursuit valuable;
- every Reference Tower has an explainable role;
- Reference and coherent alternative clear within the reviewed margin;
- the local-firepower Anti-pattern fails from spatial pressure rather than a
  hidden family penalty;
- standard fixed-speed formation remains intact;
- all eight Drafts occur at reviewed pressure nodes with a meaningful completed-
  Build window;
- exact Stage pools, Level reachability, legal Build fixtures, and Fixed
  sequences are accepted inside this Task;
- every Profile is identified as reused or first accepted here and the accepted
  Wave table matches the authored `MonsterWaveConfig`.

Fixed Draft sequences own Build comparisons; Task017 later owns natural offer
frequency and player-choice interpretation.
